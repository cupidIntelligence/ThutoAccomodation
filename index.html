House Rules & Conduct

To ensure a safe and respectful environment for every resident at Penelope 36.

01

Quiet Hours

Strict quiet hours are observed from 21:00 to 07:00 to support students' study and sleep schedules.

02

Visitor Policy

Visitors are allowed during the day but must vacate the premises by 20:00. No overnight guests without prior approval.

03

Cleanliness

Residents are responsible for cleaning their own rooms and tidying up after using shared kitchen or laundry facilities.

04

No Smoking/Alcohol

Thuto House is a smoke-free zone. Alcohol consumption is restricted to ensure a professional atmosphere.
